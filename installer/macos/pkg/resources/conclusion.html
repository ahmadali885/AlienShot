ALIENSHOT is installed

AlienShot runs from the menu bar: look for the alien icon at the top of your screen.

  • Capture: press Ctrl + Shift + S (you can change it in Options), or click the menu-bar icon and choose Take a screenshot.
  • First capture: macOS asks for Screen Recording permission. Turn on AlienShot in System Settings › Privacy & Security › Screen Recording, and reopen AlienShot if macOS asks you to.
